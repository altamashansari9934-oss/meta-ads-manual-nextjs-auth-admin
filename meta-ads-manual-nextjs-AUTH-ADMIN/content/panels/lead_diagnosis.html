Practical Diagnosis System

Campaign mein problem aa rahi hai — ab kya check aur test karna hai?

Diagnosis ka matlab sirf metric ko Good/Bad bolna nahi hai. Pehle problem ki funnel position samjho, phir focused next test choose karo.

Golden Diagnosis Rule: Ek metric ko akela dekhkar exact reason assume mat karo. Pehle us metric ko funnel ke previous aur next stage ke context mein dekho.

DIAGNOSIS CARDS

What is happening?

Ad ko enough people dekh nahi rahe.

Simple meaning

Problem creative se pehle delivery/setup side par bhi ho sakti hai.

Possible reason

Budget, audience size, campaign delivery, setup, ad approval ya competition ka impact ho sakta hai.

What to check

Budget, audience size, delivery, campaign setup, ad approval, competition.

What to test

Pehle delivery/setup check karo. Automatically new creative mat banao.

Why this test?

Low impressions ka solution automatically creative change nahi hota.

What not to change yet

Sirf low impressions dekhkar creative ko immediately replace mat karo.

What is happening?

Log ad dekh rahe hain, lekin enough people click nahi kar rahe.

Simple meaning

Delivery aa rahi hai, lekin click stage weak hai.

Possible reason

Hook, visual, message, offer ya CTA enough strong/clear nahi ho sakta.

What to check

Hook, visual, message, offer, CTA.

What to test

Stronger hook, different visual, problem/benefit message, clearer offer test karo.

Why this test?

Click stage weak hai, isliye creative/message investigate karna logical hai.

What not to change yet

Ek hi test mein audience + landing page + offer + creative sab mat badlo.

What is happening?

Bahut log first few seconds mein video chhod rahe hain.

Simple meaning

Opening attention hold nahi kar pa rahi.

Possible reason

First line weak, first visual unclear, problem clarity low, text readability issue ya opening slow ho sakti hai.

What to check

First 1–3 sec, first line, first visual, problem clarity, text readability, opening speed.

What to test

Problem / Benefit / Question hook ya new opening visual test karo.

Why this test?

Pehle opening attention improve karo.

What not to change yet

Opening signal weak hai, isliye bina evidence ke landing page ko primary problem mat samjho.

What is happening?

Starting ne attention liya, lekin 3–5 sec mein interest toot raha hai.

Simple meaning

Hook attention le raha hai; problem Hook ke baad ke content mein ho sakti hai.

Possible reason

Immediate value missing, unnecessary intro, slow pacing, ya Hook aur next line disconnected ho sakte hain.

What to check

Hook ke baad kya aa raha hai? Immediate value? Unnecessary intro? Pacing? Hook-next-line connection?

What to test

Same Hook rakho + first 3–5 sec continuation change karo.

Why this test?

Hook already attention le raha hai; pehle Hook ko change karna zaroori nahi.

What not to change yet

Hook, audience, offer aur landing page ko automatically mat badlo.

What is happening?

Beginning strong hai, middle weak ho sakta hai.

Simple meaning

Opening retain kar rahi hai, lekin deeper section mein users drop kar rahe hain.

Possible reason

Long explanation, repetition, slow pacing, same visual ya no new information.

What to check

Long explanation, repetition, slow pacing, same visual, no new information.

What to test

Shorter explanation, visual changes, demonstration, proof, filler remove karo.

Why this test?

Middle ko improve karo; opening ko bina reason mat badlo.

What not to change yet

Opening/Hook ko unnecessary replace mat karo.

What is happening?

Log video dekh rahe hain, lekin click nahi kar rahe.

Simple meaning

Retention good hai, lekin action lene ka reason weak ho sakta hai.

Possible reason

CTA, offer, benefit, reason to click ya message clarity weak ho sakti hai.

What to check

CTA, offer, benefit, reason to click, message clarity.

What to test

Stronger CTA, clearer offer, specific benefit test karo.

Why this test?

Retention good hai; action lene ka reason investigate karo.

What not to change yet

Hook ko automatically problem mat samjho.

Example

Video useful tha: “Gas leakage ke 3 signs.” Customer ne dekha. Lekin end mein sirf “Thank you for watching.”

Test: “Apne gas stove ki problem ke liye technician book karne ke liye Call Now karein.”

Simple Meaning: CTR (All) = ad par kisi bhi type ka click/action. Link CTR = specifically website/landing page link par click.

Agar CTR (All) good hai aur Link CTR low hai, to log ad ke saath interact kar rahe hain, lekin website par jaane ke liye enough reason nahi mil raha ho sakta.

What is happening?

Overall interaction aa rahi hai, lekin actual link-click intent weak hai.

Simple meaning

Engagement ko website traffic samajhna sahi nahi; Link CTR ko separately dekho.

Possible reason

CTA generic ho sakta hai, offer unclear ho sakta hai, message action tak naturally lead nahi kar raha ho sakta.

What to check

CTA, Offer, Message, Reason to Click, Creative-to-CTA connection.

What to test

Same creative + stronger CTA, clearer offer/benefit, ya stronger reason-to-click.

Why this test?

Agar attention metrics already achhe hain, to pehle action layer isolate karke test karna logical hai.

What not to change yet

Hook ko turant change mat karo agar Hook/attention metrics already achhe hain.

Example

Hook: “Gas leakage ko ignore mat karein!”

Weak CTA: “Contact Us”

Better CTA: “Apne area mein gas technician book karein — Book Now.”

Important: CTR (All) good hona website traffic good hone ki guarantee nahi hai. Link CTR actual website-click intent ko samajhne mein zyada useful hai.

What is happening?

Click aa raha hai, lekin lead conversion weak hai.

Simple meaning

Ad interest create kar raha hai; click ke baad ka part weak ho sakta hai.

Possible reason

Landing page, form, speed, trust, offer, ad-to-page message match ya tracking issue.

What to check

Landing page headline, message match, form length, page speed, trust, offer clarity, tracking.

What to test

Better landing page headline, shorter form, clearer offer, stronger trust elements.

Why this test?

Customer click kar raha hai; creative ko immediately blame mat karo.

What not to change yet

Creative/Hook ko unnecessary replace mat karo.

What is happening?

Click interest weak hai aur lead cost high hai.

Simple meaning

Lead cost problem ka signal upstream click stage par bhi dikh raha hai.

Possible reason

Hook, visual, angle, message ya offer weak ho sakta hai.

What to check

Hook, visual, angle, message, offer.

What to test

New hook + new creative angle test karo.

Why this test?

Click stage weak hai.

What not to change yet

Ek saath landing page + audience + offer + creative sab mat badlo.

What is happening?

Click aa raha hai, lekin lead expensive hai.

Simple meaning

Creative click generate kar raha hai; conversion efficiency weak ho sakti hai.

Possible reason

Landing page, form, conversion rate, offer, audience, tracking ya lead quality.

What to check

Landing page, form, conversion rate, offer, audience, tracking, lead quality.

What to test

Post-click/page/form/offer/audience tests karo.

Why this test?

Creative click generate kar raha hai; upstream creative ko unnecessarily change mat karo.

What not to change yet

CTR good hai to creative ko automatically replace mat karo.

What is happening?

Lead quantity achhi hai, lekin useful leads kam hain.

Simple meaning

Cheap ya high-volume leads business success ki guarantee nahi hain.

Possible reason

Ad message too generic, offer wrong-fit users ko attract kar raha, audience mismatch, form qualification weak.

What to check

Ad message, offer, audience, form questions, qualification.

What to test

Message ko more specific banao aur qualification improve karo.

Why this test?

Cheap lead ko business success mat samjho.

What not to change yet

Sirf CPL low dekhkar current setup ko “winner” mat declare karo.

What is happening?

Same audience ko ad zyada baar dikh raha ho sakta hai aur interest kam ho raha ho sakta hai.

Simple meaning

Creative fatigue ka signal ho sakta hai, lekin frequency alone proof nahi hai.

Possible reason

Audience repetition ke saath creative novelty/interest reduce ho raha ho sakta hai.

What to check

Frequency trend, CTR trend, audience size, spend.

What to test

New Hook, Visual, Angle ya Format test karo.

Why this test?

Frequency alone se fatigue declare mat karo; CTR trend bhi dekho.

What not to change yet

Sirf frequency high hone par fatigue final mat declare karo.

What is happening?

Attention aur clicks dono aa rahe hain, lekin conversion weak hai.

Simple meaning

Creative ka upper-funnel signal healthy hai; problem post-click conversion side par ho sakti hai.

Possible reason

Landing page, form, offer, trust, page speed ya tracking.

What to check

Landing page, form, offer, trust, page speed, tracking.

What to test

Page/form/offer/trust test karo.

Why this test?

Creative ko unnecessary replace mat karo.

What not to change yet

Hold aur CTR good hain to creative ko bina reason replace mat karo.

What is happening?

Leads aa rahi hain, lekin right customers nahi aa rahe.

Simple meaning

Lead volume theek hai, business-fit weak hai.

Possible reason

Message, offer, audience, qualification ya form wrong-fit leads attract/allow kar raha ho sakta hai.

What to check

Message, offer, audience, qualification, form.

What to test

More specific messaging aur qualification test karo.

Why this test?

Sirf CPL ko success metric mat samjho.

What not to change yet

Lead quantity ko dekhkar quality issue ignore mat karo.

Example

CPL ₹100 hai, lekin 100 leads mein sirf 15 qualified hain. Business ke liye actual problem quality ho sakti hai.

Test: Generic offer ke bajay specific customer/problem ko address karo.

Gas Service Example

0–3 sec: “Gas leakage ko ignore mat karein.”

BAD continuation: “Hum XYZ Gas Service hain aur hum pichhle 10 saal se...”

BETTER continuation: “Agar gas ki smell aa rahi hai, sabse pehle ye 3 cheezein check karein.”

Customer ne Hook par attention diya, lekin usko aage continue karne ka reason nahi mila. Isliye pehle Hook ko change karne ke bajay continuation test karo.

What to check

  • Hook ke immediately baad kya aa raha hai?
  • Kya useful information immediately mil rahi hai?
  • Kya unnecessary company introduction aa raha hai?
  • Kya video slow ho raha hai?
  • Kya Hook aur next sentence connected hain?
  • Kya customer ko continue karne ka reason mil raha hai?

What to test

  • Same Hook + new 3–5 sec continuation
  • Direct value immediately
  • Shorter sentence
  • Faster opening
  • Visual change
  • Real situation/demo

Abhi automatically kya change nahi karna?

  • Hook — agar 3-sec Hold already strong hai
  • Audience
  • Offer
  • Landing Page — jab tak data kisi aur issue ki taraf signal na kare

C

Creative Fatigue

Creative Fatigue = same audience ko same creative baar-baar dikhne ke baad interest kam hona.

Frequency high alone = fatigue nahi.

Check

  • Frequency trend
  • CTR trend
  • Audience size
  • Spend

If Frequency ↑ and CTR ↓, test

  • New Hook
  • New Visual
  • New Angle
  • New Format
D

Universal Benchmark Warning

“CTR below X = bad” ya “Landing page below Y = bad” ko rigid universal rule mat banao.

Benchmark industry, objective, audience, placement, creative, account aur campaign context ke according change ho sakta hai.

Control comparison Previous creative comparison Trend Stage-to-stage drop User-defined benchmark
Agar context insufficient ho: “Needs more context.”

Real End-to-End Example

Business: Gas servicing lead generation

Problem

3-sec Hold good hai, 5-sec Hold mein sharp drop.

Observation

Starting Hook attention le raha hai.

Diagnosis

Hook ke baad interest maintain nahi ho raha.

Check

Hook ke baad first sentence, pacing, visual, immediate value.

Control

Existing Hook + existing continuation.

Variant

Same Hook + stronger 3–5 sec continuation.

What are we testing?

Hook ke baad ka continuation.

Why?

Hook already attention le raha hai; continuation isolate karke test karna logical hai.

Learning

Agar Variant 5-sec/10-sec Hold improve karta hai, opening ke baad immediate value important ho sakti hai.

Next test

Same strong continuation ke saath different visual test karo.

Do not change yet

Audience + offer + landing page sab ek saath.
