What is happening?

Ad ko enough delivery nahi mil rahi.

Simple meaning

Ad enough people tak nahi pahunch raha.

Possible reason

Budget low ho sakta hai, audience size limited ho sakti hai, delivery/setup issue ho sakta hai, ad approval ya competition impact kar sakta hai.

What to check?

Budget, audience size, delivery, approval, campaign setup, competition.

What to test?

Pehle delivery issue diagnose karo. Budget, audience aur campaign delivery/setup ko isolate karke check karo.

Why this test?

Low impressions ka signal delivery side par hai. Sirf creative change karna automatically problem solve nahi karega.

What not to change yet?

Sirf low impressions dekhkar creative ko immediately replace mat karo.

ROAS

ROAS formula = Revenue ÷ Ad Spend.

Example: ₹50,000 revenue ÷ ₹25,000 ad spend = 2.0 ROAS.

Important: ROAS revenue-based metric hai. Profit ke liye product margin aur other costs bhi dekho.

What is happening?

ROAS achha hai, lekin total purchases limited hain.

Simple meaning

Revenue efficiency achhi ho sakti hai, lekin purchases limited hain.

Possible reason

Delivery, audience size, budget, available demand ya creative variety limited ho sakti hai.

What to check?

Delivery, audience size, budget, available demand, creative variety.

What to test?

New creative angles test ya controlled scaling consider karo; decision account data par lo.

Why this test?

Efficiency good hai, lekin volume low hai. Isliye delivery/demand/creative variety side ko diagnose karna logical hai.

What not to change yet?

Good ROAS dekhkar blindly scale mat karo; account data, available demand aur delivery context compare karo.

CPM = Cost Per 1,000 Impressions: 1,000 impressions ke liye average cost.

Why this test?

CPM aur CTR dono weak signal de rahe hain, isliye sirf creative ya sirf delivery ko isolate karke assume nahi karna chahiye.

What not to change yet?

Audience/delivery context compare kiye bina sirf ek metric ke basis par exact cause final mat karo.

Profit Formula

Profit = Revenue − Product/fulfilment costs − Ad spend − Other applicable costs.
