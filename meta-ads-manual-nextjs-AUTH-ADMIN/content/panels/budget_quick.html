Situation Library — Campaign mein fanso to diagnosis

1. Winner ka CPL suddenly badh gaya

Diagnosis: Frequency ↑? CTR ↓? Agar yes, fatigue/saturation investigate. Agar CTR stable aur landing-page CVR down, post-click investigate.
Action: Cause identify karo; phir creative/audience/funnel action.

2. Cheap CPL but bad leads

Diagnosis: Raw CPL misleading ho sakta hai.
Action: Qualified rate, cost/qualified lead, sales check; raw CPL par blindly scale nahi.

3. High CTR but no leads

Diagnosis: Attention aa rahi hai, post-click conversion weak ho sakta hai.
Action: Landing page, form, offer, speed, tracking, message match check.

4. Good leads but bad sales

Diagnosis: Ad ke baad sales process bottleneck ho sakta hai.
Action: Lead quality, follow-up, offer, qualification, CRM check.

5. New creative low CPL but little data

Diagnosis: Early signal only.
Action: Relevant data collect; premature major reallocation avoid.

6. New geo good CPL but limited data

Diagnosis: Promising but unvalidated.
Action: Quality + consistency + volume check; controlled expansion.

7. More spend but same purchases

Diagnosis: Incremental spend funnel mein kahin stop ho raha hai.
Action: Bottleneck stage identify.

8. Retargeting CPL excellent but volume tiny

Diagnosis: Small pool efficiency ko artificially strong dikha sakta hai.
Action: Audience size + frequency check; overfund mat karo.

9. Winner + new creative both good

Diagnosis: Dono ko forcefully replace/merge karna zaroori nahi.
Action: Controlled allocation + creative family.

10. Budget too small for many campaigns

Diagnosis: Fragmentation risk.
Action: Fewer meaningful tests; budget ko thin mat spread karo.

11. Too many ad sets with tiny budgets

Diagnosis: Delivery/data weak ho sakta hai.
Action: Consolidate/clear test roles.

12. One-day CPL extremely low

Diagnosis: Noise/temporary fluctuation possible.
Action: Multi-day consistency + quality check.

13. CPM rises but CTR stable

Diagnosis: Automatically creative issue nahi.
Action: Auction, audience, placement, seasonality check.

14. Frequency ↑ and CTR ↓

Diagnosis: Fatigue plausible.
Action: Creative freshness + audience size check.

15. Scaling winner damages efficiency

Diagnosis: Additional budget diminishing returns face kar sakta hai.
Action: Hold/reduce + horizontal opportunities test.

16. Average campaign not terrible

Diagnosis: Immediate kill required nahi.
Action: Controlled budget + improvement hypothesis.

17. Testing produces no winner

Diagnosis: Test design/data/offer/creative issue possible.
Action: Hypothesis, variable, delivery, data sufficiency check.

18. New audience works but quality poor

Diagnosis: Volume useful nahi if business quality drops.
Action: Message/offer/audience/qualification check.

19. New creative works, old winner still strong

Diagnosis: No need to replace prematurely.
Action: Both can coexist; monitor fatigue.

20. Client doubles budget

Diagnosis: 2x one campaign automatically mat karo.
Action: Incremental budget ko winner + testing + expansion mein strategically allocate karo.

Budget Allocation Decision Tree

•

1. Business KPI acceptable? → NO: diagnose. YES: continue.

•

2. Result consistent? → NO: more relevant data. YES: continue.

•

3. Tracking reliable? → NO: fix measurement. YES: continue.

•

4. Audience room? → YES: controlled vertical scale; LIMITED: horizontal expansion.

•

5. Creative fatigue? → YES: creative scaling/new concepts.

•

6. Post-click conversion weak? → Funnel/landing/form/checkout improvement.

•

7. New audience/geo/creative promising? → Controlled validation budget.

•

8. After change: incremental outcome monitor karo.

Diminishing Returns

Simple meaning: Budget badhne ke saath additional spend se additional result ki efficiency kam ho sakti hai.

Illustration: ₹3k → 15 qualified leads; ₹4k → 16; ₹5k → 17. Average result exist karta hai, but extra rupee ka incremental output weak ho raha hai.

Scaling decision mein average CPA ke saath marginal/incremental outcome dekho.

Budget Allocation Mistakes

•

Har campaign ko equal budget dena without role.

•

Cheap CPL/CPA ko final truth samajhna.

•

High CTR ko business success samajhna.

•

One-day data par major budget shift.

•

Winner ko blindly 5x/10x karna.

•

Too many tiny campaigns/ad sets se budget fragment karna.

•

Testing budget zero kar dena.

•

Small retargeting pool ko excessive budget dena.

•

Tracking issue ke time allocation decisions lena.

•

Budget increase ke baad incremental efficiency check na karna.

Final Framework — Performance Marketer Mindset

TOTAL BUDGET → DEFINE GOAL → ASSIGN ROLES → ALLOCATE → COLLECT DATA → DIAGNOSE FUNNEL → VALIDATE → SCALE/EXPAND → MONITOR INCREMENTAL RESULT → REALLOCATE

Budget allocation is not a one-time percentage exercise. It is a continuous capital-allocation decision based on business outcome, data quality, opportunity, capacity and risk.

Most important question: 'Agar mujhe next ₹1,000 dene hain, to woh kahan jaakar meaningful incremental business outcome create karne ka reasonable chance rakhta hai?'
