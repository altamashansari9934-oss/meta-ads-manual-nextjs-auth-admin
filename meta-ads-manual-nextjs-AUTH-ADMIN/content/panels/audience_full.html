01

Core Rule: Audience Stage ke hisaab se Creative badalta hai

Cold audience ko educate + attract karo. Warm audience ko convince karo. Hot audience ke doubts remove karke action karwao.

Same product ko har audience ko same message dikhana zaroori nahi. Audience ka previous action aur intent (kitna interested hai) decide karega ki creative ka message kitna basic ya conversion-focused hona chahiye.
02

Funnel ko simple language mein samjho

TOF

TOF – Top of Funnel

Upper funnel; user brand ko kam ya bilkul nahi jaanta.

AudienceBroad, Interest, Lookalike
Main Question: Ye kya hai aur mujhe kyun care karna chahiye?
MOF

MOF – Middle of Funnel

User interact kar chuka hai aur consideration stage mein hai.

AudienceVideo viewers, IG engagers, website visitors
Main Question: Ye mere liye relevant kyun hai?
BOF

BOF – Bottom of Funnel

User high intent par hai; conversion ke close hai.

AudienceProduct viewers, ATC, checkout, high-intent leads
Main Question: Main action kyun loon aur mera doubt kya hai?
03

Important Technical Terms

Hook

Starting attention point.

Example“₹999 mein premium oversized T-shirt?”
Angle

Product/project ko kis reason/idea se present kar rahe ho.

ExampleProblem/Solution, UGC, Quality, Price
Format

Creative ka form.

ExampleUGC video, static, carousel, demo
USP

Unique Selling Proposition; differentiate karne wali specific value.

ExampleLarge carpet area, easy exchange, premium fabric
UGC

User Generated Content; customer/creator-style natural content.

Example“Maine ye product try kiya…”
CTA

Call To Action; next step batata hai.

ExampleShop Now, Learn More, Book Site Visit
Objection

Customer ka doubt/barrier.

ExamplePrice, quality, fit, trust, payment
Seed Audience

Lookalike banane ka source audience.

ExamplePurchasers, qualified leads
04

TOF / Cold Audience: Kis type ka creative?

Cold audience tumhare brand/product ko nahi jaanti ya bahut kam jaanti hai. Primary job: attention + relevance + curiosity.

•

Strong Hook: first 1–3 seconds mein attention.

•

Problem: audience ki real problem identify karo.

•

Solution: product/project ka role clearly dikhao.

•

Proof: demo, review, actual property footage, quality, location, amenities etc.

•

Clear CTA: Learn More, Explore, View Collection, Book a Visit.

06

E-commerce TOF Creative Examples

Problem/Solution

Hook → Problem → Product solution → Demo → CTA.

Product Demo

Product ko sirf bolne ke bajaye use karke dikhao. Visual proof useful hota hai jab benefit demonstrable ho.

UGC

Natural creator-style video: “Guys, mujhe ye T-shirt Instagram par mili…” Authentic experience feel aa sakta hai.

Founder Story

“Market mein affordable premium T-shirts nahi mil rahe the, isliye humne…” — story + differentiation.

Quality + Lifestyle

Fabric, stitching, GSM, fit, packaging ya product ko gym/cafe/college/casual outing jaise contexts mein dikhao.

07

MOF / Warm Audience

Warm audience ne already kuch action kiya hai: video dekha, profile visit ki, website visit ki, product dekha etc. Focus: education + benefits + proof + objection handling.

Objection Handling

Objection = customer ka doubt/barrier. Examples: “Quality achhi hogi?”, “Size fit hoga?”, “Return milega?”, “Price itna kyun?”

•

Product benefits explain karo.

•

Reviews/testimonials dikhao.

•

FAQ creative banao.

•

Size/fit information do.

•

Comparison ya quality demo dikhao.

•

Product-page message ko ad ke message se match rakho.

08

BOF / Hot Audience

Hot audience conversion ke close hoti hai: Add to Cart, Initiate Checkout, high-intent enquiry etc. Focus: doubt removal + reason to act.

•

Genuine offer creative.

•

Reminder creative.

•

Genuine social proof.

•

Shipping/COD/return information, only if actually available.

•

Checkout/payment friction ko address karna.

New Real Estate Project: Start kaise karein?

Real Estate ka decision e-commerce product se bigger hota hai. New project ke liye Location + Project + Lifestyle + USP + Trust + Information ko progressively introduce karna useful hota hai.

TOF – Cold Real Estate Audience

•

Location: connectivity, nearby landmarks, office hubs, schools/hospitals/transport — verified information.

•

Lifestyle: exterior, living room, bedroom, balcony, amenities, actual visuals.

•

Project walkthrough: entrance, lobby, sample flat, amenities, floor plan, view.

•

Problem/Solution: real problem ko project ke verified benefits se connect karo.

•

Configuration/Price: accurate details aur required disclosures ke saath.

Remarketing vs Retargeting

Industry mein dono terms kai baar interchangeably use hote hain. Practical point: user ne pehle kya action kiya tha? Us action ke basis par next creative decide karo.

Audience actionCreative direction

Product viewerProduct benefits + objections + proof
Add to CartReminder + offer + shipping/return + trust
Initiate CheckoutPayment/checkout friction + support + trust
Existing CustomerCross-sell + upsell + repeat purchase
15

Lookalike Audience

Lookalike Audience = Meta ko existing high-quality source/seed audience dekar similar users find karne ka targeting method. Lookalike user ne tumhare brand se pehle interact kiya ho, zaroori nahi.

Isliye Lookalike ko generally acquisition/TOF mindset se treat karna useful hai.

Purchaser Lookalike

Existing purchasers ko seed bana kar similar users target kar sakte ho. Creative: UGC, product demo, benefits, proof, problem/solution.

Qualified Lead Lookalike

Lead-gen/real estate mein qualified lead data available ho to business outcome ke closer seed se Lookalike test karna conceptually useful ho sakta hai.

Important: Lookalike = warm audience nahi. User tumhare brand ko nahi jaanta ho sakta.
