01

Budget Allocation kya hota hai?

Budget = available ad-spend resource. Budget Allocation = us total resource ko campaigns, ad sets, testing, winners, audiences, geos, funnel stages, offers ya products ke beech strategic tareeke se distribute karna.

Example: Total ₹10,000/day hai. A ₹5,000, B ₹2,000, Testing ₹2,000, Retargeting ₹1,000. ₹10,000 total budget hai aur ye uska allocation hai.
Golden mindset: Budget ko sirf equal divide mat karo. Har rupee ka ROLE define karo.

05

Testing Budget vs Scaling Budget

Testing ka question: 'Kya kaam kar sakta hai?' Scaling ka question: 'Jo kaam kar raha hai usse aur acceptable volume kaise laayein?'

•

100% budget winner par daalne se future discovery ruk sakti hai.

•

100% testing par daalne se proven setup ko volume nahi mil sakta.

•

Balanced allocation current performance + future opportunity dono ko support karta hai.

SetupSimple MeaningUseful Thinking

ABO = always testing aur CBO = always scaling ko fixed rule mat samjho. Objective aur structure matter karta hai.

Lead Generation Budget Allocation

Lead Gen mein raw CPL ko final decision metric mat banao.

MetricMeaning
CPLCost per raw lead
Qualified Lead RateLeads mein useful/qualified share
Cost per Qualified LeadSpend ÷ qualified leads
Sales/BookingActual business outcome
Lead Gen hierarchy: Leads → Qualified Leads → Sales/Booking
Example: A = ₹4,000 spend, 40 leads, CPL ₹100, 4 qualified. B = ₹4,000 spend, 28 leads, CPL ₹143, 14 qualified. A ka CPL cheaper hai, lekin B ka qualified-lead economics stronger ho sakta hai.

Winner ko budget kab dena hai?

•

Business KPI acceptable ho.

•

Result one-day spike nahi, consistent direction dikha raha ho.

•

Lead/purchase quality acceptable ho.

•

Tracking reliable ho.

•

Audience mein room ho.

•

Creative/funnel stable ho.

•

Budget increase ke baad marginal efficiency collapse na kare.

Winner ≠ unlimited scaling capacity.

15

Marginal Thinking

Existing ₹3,000 ka average result aur additional ₹1,000 se milne wala EXTRA result alag cheezein hain.

Example: ₹3,000 → 15 qualified leads; ₹4,000 → 16 qualified leads. Additional spend ₹1,000, additional qualified leads 1, so marginal cost = ₹1,000 per additional qualified lead.

Average performance ke saath 'next rupee' ki productivity bhi dekho.

16

Budget Reallocation Framework

ALLOCATE → OBSERVE → DIAGNOSE → DECIDE → REALLOCATE → MONITOR
•

Allocate: Role-based initial budget do.

•

Observe: Delivery + funnel data collect karo.

•

Diagnose: Drop kis stage par hai?

•

Decide: Scale, hold, reduce, pause ya test?

•

Reallocate: Better opportunity ki taraf budget move karo.

•

Monitor: New allocation ke baad efficiency aur volume check karo.
